# 0015. Market Brain Lifecycle Boundary: Inception and Volume Milestones

## Context

Market intelligence (e.g., genre trend analysis, golden-three-chapter pacing, hook retention stats) is critical for commercial fiction. However, surfacing market alerts continuously during routine chapter writing causes cognitive fatigue, anxiety, and impulsive mid-story rewrites.

## Decision

We establish strict **Lifecycle Boundaries for Market Brain (`MarketBrief`) Proactivity**:

1. **Active Intervention Milestones**:
   Market insights are actively surfaced exclusively during:
   - *Book Inception / Premise Planning*: Genre formula matching, hook placement guidance, audience profile.
   - *Volume Planning / Volume Wrap-up*: Mid-story pacing checks, climax alignment, expectation fulfillment.
   - *Explicit Author Request*: On-demand invocation via the "Market Diagnostics" capability.

2. **Chapter-Level Drafting Silence**:
   During routine chapter drafting and scene editing, the Market Brain remains strictly silent, providing an uninterrupted, distraction-free writing environment.

## Consequences

- Authors receive maximum commercial strategic guidance when planning structure.
- Daily creative flow and immersion remain protected during chapter execution.
